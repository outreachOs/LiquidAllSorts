Belper, and everywhere around it

Liquid All Sorts is based in Belper and works right across the surrounding Peak District and North Derbyshire — from the Amber Valley towns just down the road to the Derbyshire Dales market towns further into the hills.

Below is where we regularly work. If your town isn't listed, get in touch anyway — we travel further afield for the right job.

Belper

Our home base, and a Derwent Valley Mills World Heritage town — the starting point for every job we take on.

Kilburn

A quiet village on Belper's doorstep, easily covered on the same run out.

Holbrook

Tucked between Belper and Duffield, a regular stop on our North Derbyshire rounds.

Milford

A former mill village along the Derwent, just south of Belper.

Heage

Home to Heage Windmill, one of the few working windmills left in England, and a short hop from Belper.

Crich

Best known for the National Tramway Museum, up on the hillside above the Amber Valley.

Allestree

A leafy Derby suburb built around Allestree Park, and a regular spot for car and driveway work.

Darley Abbey

A Derwent Valley Mills World Heritage village on Derby's edge, full of period stone terraces.

Matlock

The Derbyshire Dales' market town, hemmed in by the hills either side of the Derwent.

Matlock Bath

Known as the Peak District's 'seaside resort', with the Heights of Abraham cable cars overhead.

Wirksworth

A historic lead-mining town with steep stone streets and a strong well-dressing tradition.

Cromford

Home to Arkwright's Mill, the UNESCO World Heritage site that started the Derwent Valley's mill history.

Ashbourne

The southern gateway to the Peak District, with a handsome Georgian market town centre.

Bakewell

The Peak District's best-known market town — famous for its Monday market and its pudding.

Melbourne

A South Derbyshire market town whose Melbourne Hall and gardens gave their name to Melbourne, Australia.

Dronfield

A market town sitting neatly between Chesterfield and Sheffield.

Ripley

An Amber Valley town, a short run from Belper along the A610.

Duffield

Just down the valley from Belper, where the River Ecclesbourne meets the Derwent.

Not seeing your town?

We regularly travel beyond this list across Derbyshire and the Peak District — message us and we'll let you know.
