The right pressure for the right surface

Not every surface takes the same treatment — block paving, natural stone, concrete and decking all react differently under pressure. We match the technique and pressure to what's underneath, so moss, algae and years of grime lift away without pitting or stripping the surface itself.

For block paving, we can finish with re-sanded joints so the whole driveway looks properly reset, not just rinsed. It's the same care that goes into every job — done properly, not just quickly.

What's included

  • Driveways and block paving
  • Patios and natural stone
  • Decking and garden paths
  • Moss, algae and weed removal
  • Optional re-sanding of block paving joints
  • Safe technique matched to the surface
