Car Detailing

From a quick refresh to full paint correction, every car gets the same hand-finished attention — worked panel by panel until it's right.

WhatsApp Us Now Get a Free Quote

A showroom finish, panel by panel

Every car detail starts the same way — an honest look at the paintwork and interior so you know exactly what it needs, not what's easiest to sell. From there it's a full exterior wash and decontamination, wheels and arches cleaned properly, and an interior that gets a genuine deep clean rather than a quick vacuum.

If the paint needs more than a wash and wax, we can machine polish out light scratches and swirl marks before finishing with a durable sealant — or step up to a full ceramic coating if you want the protection to last.

What's included

  • Full exterior wash, decontamination and dry
  • Wheels, arches and tyres cleaned and dressed
  • Interior vacuum, deep clean and trim care
  • Glass cleaned inside and out
  • Paint refresh with wax or sealant
  • Optional machine polish for scratches and swirl marks
