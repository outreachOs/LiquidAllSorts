Motorhome & Caravan Detailing

Motorhomes and caravans need a different approach — the height, the flat panels and the algae a home wash never quite shifts. We come prepared for all of it.

WhatsApp Us Now Get a Free Quote

Built for the size of the job

Motorhomes and caravans hold onto grime differently to cars — flat panels streak, roofs get overlooked, and green algae builds up in the parts a hosepipe never reaches. We work the whole vehicle systematically, roof included, with the right brushes and reach for the height.

Awning rails, seals, steps and the towbar all get proper attention too, since those are usually the first places to show neglect. The result is a motorhome or caravan that's genuinely ready for its next trip, not just rinsed off.

What's included

  • Full roof wash and inspection
  • Awning rail and seals cleaned
  • Algae and green staining removed from panels
  • Wheel arches, steps and towbar cleaned
  • Windows, mirrors and lights detailed
  • Ready-for-the-road finish, inside and out
