A quick tip before you dive in: reviews and small wording tweaks are fine to update whenever you like, but try not to change your WhatsApp number, service names, or make big overhauls more than once every 2–3 weeks. Google builds up trust in your business listing over time, and constantly changing the core details can confuse that and hurt how consistently you show up in local search. Small, steady updates beat frequent big ones.
